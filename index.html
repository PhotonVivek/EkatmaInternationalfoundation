Ekatma International Foundation is dedicated to spreading the timeless wisdom of Adi Sankaracharya and promoting harmony, spiritual understanding, cultural values, and universal oneness.

About Ekatma International Foundation

The Ekatma International Foundation aims to preserve, promote, and disseminate the spiritual and philosophical teachings of Adi Sankaracharya. The foundation works towards building a society rooted in unity, wisdom, compassion, and cultural heritage.

Who We Are

We are a collective of scholars, educators, spiritual seekers, social workers, and volunteers committed to advancing the ideals of Ekatma — the realization of oneness among all beings.

Philosophy of Sankaracharya (Ekatma Philosophy)

Ekatma Philosophy emphasizes the essential unity of existence. Rooted in Advaita Vedanta, Adi Sankaracharya taught that the individual self and the universal consciousness are fundamentally one. This philosophy inspires peace, harmony, and spiritual awakening.
